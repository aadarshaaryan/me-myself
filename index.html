  • Me & Myself ❤️

  • Sometimes, when everything becomes quiet, I find myself talking to the only person who is always there — me.
  • I talk to myself when I have too many thoughts and nowhere to put them. I ask questions I don't know how to answer, and sometimes I answer them anyway.
  • "Why are you worried?"
    "What are you trying to prove?"
    "Are you actually tired, or are you just scared of not being good enough?"
  • There are moments when I laugh at my own thoughts. There are also moments when I sit quietly, staring at nothing, trying to understand what is happening inside my head.
  • I know myself better than anyone else ever could. I remember every little failure I don't talk about, every dream I haven't told anyone, and every small achievement I quietly felt proud of.
  • And yet, sometimes, I am also the person who is hardest on me.
  • I expect myself to know everything, to improve quickly, to never waste time, and to keep moving even when I don't feel like moving.
  • So lately, I'm trying something different.
  • Instead of asking, "Why aren't you better yet?", I'm trying to ask, "How far have you already come?"
  • Instead of being angry at myself for making mistakes, I'm trying to understand why I made them.
  • Maybe talking to yourself isn't always loneliness. Maybe sometimes it's simply a way of listening to the person you've been ignoring while trying to keep up with everything else.
  • There are conversations I'll probably never have with anyone.
  • But I hope I never stop having them with myself.
  • Because through all the noise, all the expectations, all the confusion, and all the unfinished dreams...
  • I still have me.
  • And maybe, for now, that's enough. ❤️
